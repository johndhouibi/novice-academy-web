# Novice Academy Hub

Build a modern, welcoming, and fully responsive website for an educational and training center called "Novice Academy" located in Sfax, Tunisia (Route Tunis km 1 Ariana). 

### Target Audience:
- Parents looking for extra tutoring and academic support for their children.
- Students and adults looking for professional skill development, language courses, and creative workshops.

### Design & Theme:
- Color Palette: Shades of pink and crisp white for a clean, elegant, and friendly aesthetic.
- Style: Professional yet creative, warm, modern, and easy to navigate on both mobile and desktop.

### Key Sections & Pages Needed:
1. Homepage:
   - A captivating hero section highlighting language courses, tutoring, and workshops with a clear Call to Action (CTA) like "Book a Course" or "Contact Us".
   - Quick overview of who we are and what we offer.
2. Courses & Trainings:
   - Language Courses: English, French, Spanish, and Italian.
   - Academic Tutoring: Extra support for school students.
   - Professional & Creative Workshops (based on our academy's offerings): 
     * Crochet & Art Déco
     * Pearl Bags (Sacs en Perle)
     * Embroidery (Broderie)
     * Pastry & French Desserts (Pâtisserie & الحلويات الفرنسية)
     * Ice Cream, Juice, and Milkshake Making
3. About Us:
   - Mission and vision of Novice Academy in Sfax, focusing on high-quality learning and hands-on skill-building.
4. Contact & Location:
   - Address: Route Tunis km 1 Ariana, Sfax, Tunisia, 3000
   - Phone: 52 644 706
   - Email: noviceacademy05@gmail.com
   - Social Media: Link to Facebook page (Novice Academy)
   - An interactive contact/registration form.
